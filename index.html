Important notice — please read carefully
1. Under the Strata Property Act, a tenant must comply with the bylaws and rules of the strata corporation in force from time to time.

2. The current bylaws and rules may be changed by the strata corporation, and if changed, the tenant must comply with the changed bylaws and rules.

3. If a tenant, occupant, or visitor contravenes a bylaw or rule, the tenant is responsible and may be subject to penalties including fines, denial of access to recreational facilities, and payment of remediation costs.

Make.com setup instructions

Make.com handles all email sending automatically. Create these 3 scenarios in Make.com:

1
Tenant signing email

Trigger: Airtable — Watch Records (new record created)
Action: Gmail — Send email
To: Tenant Email · Subject: Please sign Form K — Unit [Unit Number]
Body: include Tenant Name and Tenant Signing Links field

2
Owner countersign email

Trigger: Airtable — Watch Records (filter: Tenants Signed Count = Tenant Count)
Action: Gmail — Send email
To: Owner Email · Subject: Please countersign Form K — Unit [Unit Number]
Body: include Owner Name and Owner Signing Link field

3
Council notification

Trigger: Airtable — Watch Records (filter: Both Signed = checked)
Action: Gmail — Send email
To: info@rivendelleps87.ca and president@rivendelleps87.ca
Body: Unit Number, Tenants, Owner Name, Owner Email
